Suggested Daily Routine (4–5 Hours)

Divide your study hours around work or daily commitments

Time Slot Module Core Activity
Morning / Commute Listening & Speaking Listen to BBC Podcasts or a Listening test on earphones; brainstorm Speaking topics mentally.
Evening / Early Night Reading (1.5 Hours) Take 1 timed Reading test and conduct a deep Mistake Analysis of every wrong answer.
Late Night Writing & Speaking (1.5 Hours) Write 1 Writing task and practice Speaking in front of a mirror or record audio on your phone.
